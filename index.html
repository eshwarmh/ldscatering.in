Our Story

Family recipes, cooked for your family's biggest days

Sri Annapurneshwari Catering began as a small kitchen serving neighbourhood weddings, and has grown — one function at a time — into a trusted name for families across Karnataka and Andhra Pradesh.

Every menu is built the traditional way: fresh grinding, slow-cooked gravies, and the kind of banana-leaf spread that makes guests ask for seconds.

  • On-time setup and service, guaranteed
  • Menus customised to region, community & guest count
  • Trained staff for cooking, serving & cleanup
