FAQ

Common Questions
Answered

Do you cater weddings?

+

Yes! Wedding catering is our specialty. We offer grand traditional feasts with authentic South Indian flavors. From pre-wedding rituals to the reception, we make your big day delicious and memorable.

Can the menu be customized?

+

Absolutely! We customize every menu to match your taste, budget, and event type. Whether you want traditional dishes or modern fusion, we create a menu that perfectly suits your needs.

How many guests can you serve?

+

We can cater events of all sizes — from intimate gatherings of 50 guests to large celebrations with 1000+ guests. Our team is equipped to handle any scale with premium quality.

Do you travel outside Chintamani?

+

Yes, we provide catering services across Chikkaballapur district and beyond. We can travel to Bengaluru, Kolar, Hosakote, and other nearby areas. Contact us to discuss your location.

Do you provide serving staff?

+

Yes, we provide professional serving staff for all events. Our team is well-trained, courteous, and ensures flawless service throughout your event.

What types of events do you cater?

+

We cater weddings, receptions, birthdays, corporate events, temple functions, housewarming ceremonies, naming ceremonies, engagements, anniversaries, outdoor events, and festivals.

How do I book your services?

+

Simply call us at +91 99803 40771 or message us on WhatsApp. We'll discuss your event, customize a menu, and finalize the booking.

What is your pricing?

+

Our pricing depends on the event type, number of guests, menu selection, and location. We offer affordable packages without compromising on quality. Call us for a customized quote.
